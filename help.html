Getting started开始使用

1. Add OneScroll to Control Center

1. 把一滑长图加进控制中心

You only need to do this once.

只要加一次。

  1. Swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen to open Control Center.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area, then tap Add a Control at the bottom.
  3. Search for OneScroll and tap Long Screenshot to add it.
  1. 从屏幕右上角往下拉，打开控制中心。
  2. 长按空白处，再点底部的「添加控制」。
  3. 搜「一滑长图」或「OneScroll」，点「截长图」把它加进来。

Names in Control Center are set by iOS and follow your iPhone's language. On an iPhone set to Chinese, search for 一滑长图.

控制中心里的名字由系统决定，跟着手机的语言。手机是英文的话，要搜「OneScroll」，按钮叫「Long Screenshot」。

2. Take a long screenshot

2. 截一张长图

  1. Open what you want to capture and stay at the point where the image should begin.
  2. Open Control Center and tap Long Screenshot. (Or tap the big button in OneScroll, then switch to that app.)
  3. When iOS asks what to share, choose your entire screen.
  4. Scroll down at an even pace, in one direction. Pause briefly where the image should end.
  5. Tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen and choose Stop. You can also tap Long Screenshot in Control Center again.
  1. 打开要截的内容，停在想要的开头。
  2. 打开控制中心，点「截长图」。（也可以在一滑长图里点大圆按钮，再切过去。）
  3. 系统问要共享什么时，选「整个屏幕」。
  4. 匀速往下滑，只往一个方向。滑到想要的地方停一下。
  5. 点屏幕顶上的红色录屏标志，选「停止」。也可以再点一次控制中心里的「截长图」。

A few seconds later the long screenshot is saved to Photos, and you get a notification.

停下几秒后，长图自动存进相册，还会来一条通知。

3. Crop or share

3. 裁切、分享

  1. Tap the notification, or open OneScroll and tap Last capture.
  2. Tap the image where you want to crop. Drag the red line into place, then choose Start here or End here. The orange lines can be dragged to fine-tune.
  3. Tap Save to Photos (this adds a new copy) or Share.
  1. 点那条通知，或者打开一滑长图点「上一张长图」。
  2. 点一下图上要裁开的地方，上下拖动红线对准，再选「从这里开始」或「到这里结束」。橙色线可以拖动微调。
  3. 点「存到相册」（会另存一张新的）或「分享」。

Something's not right遇到问题

Part of the page is missing, or the image stops early长图缺了一段，或者没拼到最后

This usually means the page moved too far between two moments, for example after a quick flick. Scroll steadily at a moderate speed, only downward, and pause briefly at the end before you stop.

一般是某一下滑得太快（比如用力一甩），两个画面之间跳得太远，接不上。请匀速、不快不慢地往下滑，不要往回滑，滑到最后停一下再点停止。

If you switch to another page or app during a capture, OneScroll keeps the stretch where you scrolled the most.

截图过程中如果换到了别的页面或 App，一滑长图会留下滑动最多的那一段。

It says it couldn't make a long screenshot提示「没拼成」

OneScroll didn't see the page scroll. Make sure you chose your entire screen in the sharing sheet, then scrolled within one page. Very short scrolls may not be enough.

说明一滑长图没看到页面在滑动。请确认在系统的共享界面里选了「整个屏幕」，然后在同一个页面里往下滑。滑得太短也可能拼不出来。

If iOS interrupts the recording, the part captured so far is still saved.

如果录屏被系统中断，已经拼好的部分照样会保存。

Some areas are black有些地方是黑的

Some videos and password fields record as black. That's iOS protecting them, and no screen recording app can capture them.

有些视频和密码输入框录下来是黑的，这是 iOS 在保护它们，任何录屏 App 都录不到。

Videos or animations look frozen视频、动图变成了静止的

A long screenshot is a still image, so videos and animations that play while you scroll end up as a single frame.

长图是一张静态图片，滑动时正在播放的视频、动画只会留下一个画面。

A notification banner ended up in the image通知横幅被截进去了

While you share your screen, iOS records everything on it, including notifications. Turn on Do Not Disturb in Control Center before you start.

共享屏幕时，iOS 会录下屏幕上的一切，包括弹出来的通知。开始前在控制中心打开「勿扰模式」就不会了。

Where are the time and battery at the top?顶上的时间、电量去哪了？

The status bar is trimmed by default, so the image starts with your content. To keep it, open OneScroll, go to Settings and turn on Keep status bar. It applies from your next capture. The Dynamic Island and recording indicator are always removed.

顶部状态栏默认去掉，长图从内容开始。想留着的话，打开一滑长图，在「设置」里打开「保留顶部状态栏」，下一次截图开始生效。灵动岛和录屏红点总会抹掉。

The long screenshot isn't in Photos相册里找不到长图

OneScroll needs permission to add photos. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → OneScroll and choose Add Photos Only. Until then, the image stays in the app and is saved once you allow access.

一滑长图需要「添加照片」的权限。到「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 一滑长图」，选「仅添加照片」。在这之前长图先留在 App 里，允许以后就会存进去。

I don't get a notification没有收到通知

Go to Settings → Notifications → OneScroll and turn on Allow Notifications. The long screenshot is saved either way.

到「设置 → 通知 → 一滑长图」，打开「允许通知」。不开通知也不影响长图保存。

I can't find OneScroll when adding a control添加控制时搜不到

The name follows your iPhone's language: try both OneScroll and 一滑长图. If it still doesn't appear, open OneScroll once, then try again.

名字跟着手机的语言，「一滑长图」和「OneScroll」都搜一下。还是没有的话，先打开一次一滑长图，再回去搜。

Can I scroll up or sideways?可以往上滑或者横着滑吗？

Not at the moment. Start at the top of what you want and scroll down.

现在还不行。请从想要的开头开始，往下滑。

Which devices are supported?支持哪些设备？

iPhone with iOS 27 or later. Capturing your whole screen while you use other apps is new in iOS 27, so earlier versions can't run OneScroll. iPad isn't supported yet.

需要 iOS 27 或更新版本的 iPhone。在别的 App 里持续录屏是 iOS 27 才有的能力，所以更早的系统用不了。暂时不支持 iPad。

Is anything I capture uploaded?截的内容会上传吗？

No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. OneScroll has no account, no analytics and no server, and it keeps only your most recent long screenshot so you can crop or share it. See the Privacy Policy.

不会。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。一滑长图没有账号、没有统计、没有服务器，只保留最近一张长图方便你裁切、分享。详见隐私政策。

Purchases购买

How much can I use it for free?能免费用多少？

Your first 5 long screenshots are free. After that, you can unlock the full version with a one-time purchase and capture as many as you like. The price is shown in the app.

前 5 张长图免费。之后一次性购买就能解锁完整版，想截多少截多少。价格在 App 里显示。

Is it a subscription?是订阅吗？

No. You pay once. Nothing renews and you won't be charged again.

不是。一次买断，不会自动续费，也不会再扣钱。

I got a new iPhone or reinstalled the app换了新 iPhone，或者重装了 App

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open OneScroll, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

登录同一个 Apple 账户，打开一滑长图，在「设置」里点「恢复购买」。

I paid, but it's still locked付了钱还是没解锁

Tap Restore purchases in Settings. If the purchase is waiting for approval (for example, Ask to Buy from a parent), it unlocks automatically once approved.

在「设置」里点「恢复购买」。如果购买在等批准（比如需要家长同意），批准以后会自动解锁。

Can my family use my purchase?家人能共用我的购买吗？

No, Family Sharing isn't available for this purchase.

不能，这项购买不支持家人共享。

How do I get a refund?怎么退款？

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

购买由 Apple 处理，退款也由 Apple 处理，可以在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。
