How to stitch screenshots together on iPhone

By the OneScroll team · Updated November 2026

iPhone has no one-tap way to stitch screenshots, and the Photos app can't merge them. You can stack them with the Combine Images action in the Shortcuts app, use a stitching app that lines up the overlapping parts, or skip stitching by capturing one long screenshot while you scroll with OneScroll.

Option 1: Combine Images in the Shortcuts app

  1. Open Shortcuts and create a new shortcut.
  2. Add Select Photos and allow selecting more than one.
  3. Add Combine Images and set it to combine vertically.
  4. Add Save to Photo Album, then run the shortcut and pick your screenshots in order.

This is free and built in, but it only stacks the images as they are. It doesn't remove overlapping parts or the status bar at the top of each screenshot, so take each screenshot where the last one ended, or crop them first.

Option 2: A stitching app

Stitching apps look for the part two screenshots have in common and join them there. They work well when every screenshot overlaps the next by a few lines and nothing on screen changed in between. You still have to take all the screenshots first.

Option 3: Skip the stitching

If the content is still on your screen, you don't need separate screenshots at all. OneScroll captures while you scroll and joins the pieces as it goes.

  1. Once only: add the Long Screenshot control to Control Center. Swipe down from the top-right corner, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control, then search for OneScroll.
  2. Open what you want to capture and stay where the image should begin. Open Control Center, tap Long Screenshot, and choose to share your entire screen.
  3. Scroll down at an even pace. When you reach the end, tap the red recording indicator at the top of the screen and choose Stop.

A few seconds later the long screenshot is in Photos as one image, and you get a notification. Tap it to crop the start and end or to share.

Tips for cleaner manual stitching

  • Leave a few lines of overlap between screenshots, so there is something to match.
  • Don't change text size, zoom or light and dark mode partway through.
  • Wait for banners and pop-ups to go away before each screenshot.
  • Take the screenshots in order. Photos keeps them sorted by time, which makes picking them easier.

Common questions

Can the Photos app stitch screenshots?

No. Photos can crop and mark up a screenshot, but it can't merge several into one image.

What is the fastest way to combine screenshots of a long page?

Not taking separate screenshots in the first place. For two or three screens, stitching is fine. For a long chat or page, capturing while you scroll is quicker and avoids missing pieces.

Can I stitch screenshots I took earlier?

Yes, with Shortcuts or a stitching app. OneScroll doesn't combine existing screenshots; it captures a page that is on your screen now.

Why do my stitched screenshots have repeated lines?

The screenshots overlap and the tool stacked them without removing the overlap. Use a stitching app that matches the overlapping part, or crop each screenshot first.
