OneScroll Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

OneScroll turns what you scroll through on your iPhone into one long screenshot. It is built so that nothing about you or your screen leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. OneScroll has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no server. The only connection the app makes is to Apple's App Store, for in-app purchases.

Screen recording

  • When you start a capture, iOS shares your screen with OneScroll until you stop it.
  • Frames are stitched in memory on your iPhone. They are never uploaded and never written to storage. Only the finished long screenshot is kept.

Photos

OneScroll asks only for permission to add photos, so it can save your long screenshots. It cannot see or read your photo library.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your iPhone to tell you when a long screenshot is ready. No data is sent anywhere.

Stored on your iPhone

OneScroll keeps only your most recent long screenshot (so you can crop or share it), your settings, and how many free long screenshots you have made. You can delete the screenshot from the home screen. Deleting the app removes everything except that count: it is kept in your iPhone's keychain, so reinstalling the app does not reset the free screenshots. It never leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

OneScroll is free to try for a few long screenshots, then offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks it for good. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple and verified on your iPhone. We never receive your payment details or any information about your Apple Account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email onescroll.help@gmail.com.

一滑长图隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

一滑长图把你在 iPhone 上滑过的内容拼成一张长图。它从设计上就不让你的任何信息、任何画面离开这台设备。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。一滑长图没有账号、没有统计、没有广告、没有追踪。我们没有服务器，App 唯一的联网是连 Apple 的 App Store 处理 App 内购买。

录屏

  • 开始截图时，iOS 会把屏幕共享给一滑长图，直到你停止。
  • 画面只在这台 iPhone 的内存里拼接，不上传，也不写进存储。只留下最后拼好的长图。

相册

一滑长图只申请「仅添加照片」权限，用来保存长图。它看不到、也读不了你的相册。

通知

通知在你的 iPhone 上生成，用来告诉你长图拼好了，不会发送任何数据。

存在你 iPhone 上的东西

一滑长图只保留最近一张长图（方便你裁切、分享）、你的设置，以及你已经免费截了几张。长图可以在首页删掉。删除 App 会清除这些内容，只有免费张数例外：它存在 iPhone 的钥匙串里，重装 App 不会重置免费张数。它不会离开你的 iPhone。

购买

一滑长图可以免费截几张长图，之后提供一次性的 App 内购买，买了永久解锁。购买全部由 Apple 处理，并在你的 iPhone 上验证。我们收不到你的任何付款信息，也收不到你 Apple 账户的任何信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在这个页面，并更新生效日期。

对本政策或 App 有任何问题，请发邮件到 onescroll.help@gmail.com。
