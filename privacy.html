OneScroll Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

OneScroll turns what you scroll through on your iPhone into one long screenshot. It is built so that nothing about you or your screen leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. OneScroll has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Screen recording

  • When you start a capture, iOS shares your screen with OneScroll until you stop it.
  • Frames are stitched in memory on your iPhone. They are never uploaded and never written to storage. Only the finished long screenshot is kept.

Photos

OneScroll asks only for permission to add photos, so it can save your long screenshots. It cannot see or read your photo library.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your iPhone to tell you when a long screenshot is ready. No data is sent anywhere.

Stored on your iPhone

OneScroll keeps only your most recent long screenshot (so you can crop or share it) and your settings. You can delete the screenshot from the home screen, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases

If OneScroll offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

一滑长图隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

一滑长图把你在 iPhone 上滑过的内容拼成一张长图。它从设计上就不让你的任何信息、任何画面离开这台设备。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。一滑长图没有账号、没有统计、没有广告、没有追踪，也不联网。

录屏

  • 开始截图时，iOS 会把屏幕共享给一滑长图，直到你停止。
  • 画面只在这台 iPhone 的内存里拼接，不上传，也不写进存储。只留下最后拼好的长图。

相册

一滑长图只申请「仅添加照片」权限，用来保存长图。它看不到、也读不了你的相册。

通知

通知在你的 iPhone 上生成，用来告诉你长图拼好了，不会发送任何数据。

存在你 iPhone 上的东西

一滑长图只保留最近一张长图（方便你裁切、分享）和你的设置。长图可以在首页删掉；删除 App 会清除所有内容。

购买

如果一滑长图提供 App 内购买，全部由 Apple 处理，我们收不到你的任何付款信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在这个页面，并更新生效日期。
