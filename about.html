About OneScroll

OneScroll is a small iPhone app made by an independent developer. It does one job: turn a scroll into one long screenshot.

What it does

Start it from Control Center, scroll through a chat, a post or a web page, and stop. OneScroll stitches while you scroll and saves the whole thing to Photos as one image, with the Dynamic Island and recording dot erased. It works in almost any app, including the many where iPhone's built-in Full Page option doesn't appear.

How it's built

Everything happens on your iPhone. There is no server, no account, no ads and no tracking, and the app collects no data. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

What it costs

Your first 5 long screenshots are free. After that, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks the full version: US$2.99 in the US, and the App Store shows the local price elsewhere. No subscription, nothing renews.

Versions

OneScroll 1.0 came out on the App Store in November 2026. It needs an iPhone with iOS 27 or later.
