OneScroll

Scroll once, get one long screenshot. Start from Control Center in any app, scroll, and stop. The long screenshot is saved to Photos. Everything happens on your iPhone.

一滑长图：在任何 App 里从控制中心开始，滑完停下，长图自动存进相册。全部在你的 iPhone 上完成。
