One scroll.
One long screenshot.

滑一次，
就是一整张长图。

Tap once in Control Center, scroll, then stop. OneScroll stitches as you go, so the whole chat, article or product page lands in Photos as a single image.

在控制中心点一下，往下滑，再停下。一滑长图边滑边拼，整段聊天、整篇文章、整个商品页，几秒后就作为一张图存进相册。

Coming soon to the App Store 即将登陆 App Store

For iPhone · Requires iOS 27 or later

适用于 iPhone · 需要 iOS 27 或更新版本

Tap, scroll, stop.

点一下，滑一滑，停下。

Start right from the page you want. No need to open OneScroll first.

在要截的页面直接开始，不用先打开一滑长图。

1

Add it to Control Center

加进控制中心

You only do this once. Swipe down from the top-right corner, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control, then search for OneScroll.

只要加一次。从右上角往下拉出控制中心，长按空白处，点「添加控制」，搜「一滑长图」或「OneScroll」。

2

Tap Long Screenshot

点「截长图」

On the page you want, open Control Center and tap Long Screenshot. When iOS asks what to share, choose your entire screen.

停在要截的页面开头，拉出控制中心点「截长图」。系统问要共享什么时，选「整个屏幕」。

3

Scroll, then stop

滑完，点停止

Scroll down at an even pace. Tap the red recording indicator and choose Stop. A few seconds later it's in Photos, and you get a notification.

匀速往下滑。滑完点屏幕顶上的红色录屏标志，选「停止」。几秒后长图就在相册里，还会来一条通知。
