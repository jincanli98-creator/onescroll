How to choose a long screenshot app for iPhone

By the OneScroll team · Updated November 2026

Check the joins first: a good app lines up each part so that no line appears twice or goes missing. Then make sure the Dynamic Island and the recording dot don't end up in the image, that no screen recording is left behind in Photos, that it works in the apps you use, and that the price and privacy suit you. Below is what to look for, and how OneScroll handles each point.

1. Clean joins

A long screenshot is many screens joined together. If an app joins two parts in the wrong place, a line of text or a whole message appears twice, or a piece goes missing. It is the most common flaw, and the easiest to miss until you read the image closely. Before you rely on an app, take a test capture and zoom in on a few joins.

OneScroll lines up each new part with what it has already stitched, so the text at the joins doesn't repeat. Scrolling at an even pace gives the cleanest result.

2. Nothing left at the top

Apps that build the image from a screen recording start from what the recording shows, including the status bar, the Dynamic Island and the red recording indicator. Some leave them in, so every long screenshot starts with a strip you have to crop.

OneScroll erases the Dynamic Island and the recording dot, and removes the status bar by default. If you want the time and battery at the top, like a normal screenshot, turn on Keep status bar in Settings.

3. No recording left behind

Some apps work from a screen recording you make first. The video is saved in Photos, so after every capture there is a recording to delete, and long recordings take up space.

OneScroll stitches while you scroll and saves only the finished image. No video is kept.

4. Works in the apps you use

iPhone's built-in Full Page option only appears in some apps, such as Safari (see where Full Page works). A capture app should work wherever you scroll: chats, social feeds, shopping pages and documents. Whatever you use, some videos and password fields are protected by iOS and come out black.

OneScroll captures your entire screen while you scroll, so it works in almost any app that scrolls up and down.

5. A price that suits you

Long screenshot apps come free with ads, free with paid extras, by subscription, or as a one-time purchase. If you only need a long screenshot now and then, a subscription is easy to forget about. Check what the free version can really do before you pay.

OneScroll has no ads and no subscription. Your first 5 long screenshots are free; after that a one-time purchase (US$2.99 in the US) unlocks unlimited use.

6. Your screen stays private

Long screenshots often hold private chats. Check the privacy label on the app's App Store page, and whether the app needs an account or uploads your images to process them.

OneScroll stitches everything on your iPhone, uploads nothing, needs no account and collects no data. It only asks for permission to add photos, not to read your library. See the Privacy Policy.

When something else fits better

  • A webpage in Safari: the built-in Full Page option is quick and free. See how to take a long screenshot on iPhone.
  • Screenshots you have already taken: OneScroll captures while you scroll and can't combine existing images. Use Combine Images in Shortcuts or a stitching app; see how to stitch screenshots together.
  • Pages that scroll sideways, an iPad, or an iPhone that can't run iOS 27: OneScroll only covers scrolling up and down on an iPhone with iOS 27 or later.

Common questions

What is the best long screenshot app for iPhone?

It depends on what you capture. For webpages in Safari, the built-in Full Page option is enough. For chats, feeds and pages in other apps, choose an app with clean joins that leaves nothing at the top of the image, and whose price suits how often you use it. OneScroll is our app, and it was built around those points.

Why does my long screenshot have repeated lines?

The app joined two parts in the wrong place. Scrolling at an even pace helps, and some apps line up the joins more reliably than others. Test a long chat and zoom in on the joins.

Why is the Dynamic Island in my long screenshot?

The image was built from a screen recording, which shows the status bar and the recording indicator. Crop the top, or use an app that erases them, such as OneScroll.

Do I need to keep a screen recording to make a long screenshot?

Not with OneScroll. It stitches while you scroll and saves only the image. Apps that work from a recording save the video in Photos, and you can delete it afterwards.
